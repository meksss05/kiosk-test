Mumbai RTO — open-world street, lots and kiosk hall

प्रादेशिक परिवहन कार्यालय, मुंबई

Walk the wet street, the kaali-peeli and auto stand, the truck yard and the RTO hall. Six Sarathi self-service kiosks run live sessions for people in the queues, two help-centre counters serve the sides, and a waiting area seats everyone else. The wall clocks show your real local time and the hall fills at real peak hours (about 10:30–13:00 and 14:30–16:30). Drag the time slider to preview any hour.

Press E next to a taxi, auto, van, police car or truck to drive it. Park it in a marked bay. Click a kiosk screen in the lobby to try the Sarathi screens yourself.
